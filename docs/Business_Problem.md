# Business Problem

## Project Background

A retail e-commerce company wants to analyze its sales performance to improve business decisions.

The company sells products across different regions, categories, and customer segments.

Management currently has raw sales data but lacks a centralized dashboard to monitor business performance.

---

## Project Objective

Build an interactive sales dashboard that helps management:

- Monitor total sales and profit
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Compare regional performance
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## Expected Outcome

The dashboard should provide clear insights that help management improve sales, profitability, and operational efficiency.
